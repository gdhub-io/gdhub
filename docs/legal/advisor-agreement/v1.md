## 1. Definitions

**Advisor Space** — the part of the Platform available to a user who has accepted this
Agreement: the request inbox, the reviews, and the advisor settings.

**Consultation** — a single session, written answer or review that you give to another
user through the Platform, whether free or paid.

**Checkpoint** — the 28-day program the Platform sells to a project author, described in
the Checkpoint Standard published on gdhub.io.

**Curation** — running one Checkpoint: the five consultations, the written answers to
four devlogs, the goal card and the result card with the verdict.

**Author** — the user who buys a Checkpoint or requests a Consultation.

**Checkpoint Standard** — the published rules of the program (calendar, duties,
verdicts, deadlines), as in force on the day a given Checkpoint starts.

**Fee** — the amount payable to you for a paid Consultation or for Curation.

**Volunteer Engagement** — an engagement you accept without a Fee, under Section 4.

## 4. Volunteer Engagements (unpaid)

1. You may give Consultations and run Checkpoints **without a Fee**. The Platform is a
   non-profit organisation and may engage you as a volunteer under the Republic of

2. A Volunteer Engagement carries **no remuneration in any form** — no fee, no equity, no
   revenue share, no claim to the Author's project.
3. Volunteering is not work under employment law and creates no employment rights.
4. We may reimburse documented direct expenses agreed in advance. Reimbursement is not a
   Fee.
5. You may stop a Volunteer Engagement at any time by telling us. If a Checkpoint is

6. At your request we will conclude a separate written volunteering agreement, as the
   Law on Volunteering allows.

## 5. Paid Consultations

1. You set the price of your Consultations in your advisor settings, in {{entity.currency}}. The Platform
   shows the price to the Author before they book.
2. The Author pays the Platform. The Platform pays you the price you set **less the
   platform fee** published in the advisor settings at the time of booking.
3. A Consultation counts as delivered when it has taken place, or when the Author did not
   attend after a confirmed booking and you were available.
4. Either side may cancel a booked Consultation at least **12 hours** in advance without
   consequence. Later cancellation by you means no Fee for it; later cancellation by the
   Author means the Fee is payable to you.
5. You may switch between free and paid at any time. The change applies to new bookings
   only.

## 6. Curation of a Checkpoint

1. When you take a Checkpoint you accept the **Checkpoint Standard** as in force on the
   day that Checkpoint starts, and it forms part of this Agreement for that Checkpoint.
2. In summary, and without replacing the Standard, for each Checkpoint you owe:
   - the goal card agreed with the Author on day 1, cut to what 28 days hold;
   - four consultations of 45 minutes on the days set by the Standard;
   - a written answer to each of the four devlogs;
   - your presence at the shared gathering, where you answer your own teams;
   - the result card and the verdict, published **by day 30**.
3. **Take only as many Checkpoints as you can run without missing a call.** Capacity is
   your own judgement and your own responsibility.
4. Two consultations missed in a row without agreement, or a verdict not published by day
   30, entitle the Platform to replace you inside that Checkpoint. Where that happens, the
   Fee for the parts you did not perform is not payable, and any amount already paid for
   them is set off against your next payment or refunded to us.
5. The Fee for Curation is calculated from your hourly rate and the size of the team, as
   published in the price list, and is payable after the verdict is published.
6. The Platform may run a Checkpoint with its **AI curator** instead of a person. That is
   a different product and does not affect the Checkpoints you run.

## 8. How you work

1. **Give honest judgement.** You say what you actually think about the state of the
   project, including on day 14 when the scope will not fit.
2. **You do not promise an outcome.** You do not guarantee that a goal will be met, that a
   game will be finished, funded, published or sold.
3. **No conflict of interest, or disclose it.** Tell us and the Author before you take a
   Checkpoint or a Consultation if you have an interest in the project, its competitor,
   or in a service you would be recommending. You may not take a commission from a third
   party for a recommendation you make in the advisor role.
4. **Do not sell during the engagement.** You may not use a Consultation or a Checkpoint
   to sell your own products, courses or services, or to solicit an investment or an
   equity share in the Author's project.
5. **Conduct.** Harassment, discrimination, threats, and any conduct prohibited by the
   Terms of Use end the engagement immediately.
6. **You perform personally.** You may not hand a Checkpoint to someone else without our
   written agreement.

## 9. Your own experience, and the use of AI

**What an Author buys from you is your own experience.** They come to you because you have
made games, shipped them, run teams, failed at things and know why. The judgement in your
answers has to be yours: what you have actually seen work, and what you have watched go
wrong. That is the whole value of the advisor role, and the Platform already offers an AI
curator to anyone who wants generated feedback instead.

1. **The judgement is yours.** Every answer, card and verdict you publish must rest on
   your own assessment of that project. You may not publish, as your advice, output you
   have not thought through and cannot stand behind.
2. **AI is a tool for the form, not a substitute for the judgement.** You may use AI tools
   to draft, structure, translate or tidy your writing. You remain fully responsible for
   everything published under your name and must review it before it goes out.
3. **Say so where it matters.** Where a written answer, a card or a verdict is
   **substantially generated by an AI tool**, you say so in it. Passing generated text off
   as your own reviewed judgement is a serious breach of this Agreement and ends the
   engagement.
4. **Confidential material stays out of public AI services.** You may not upload an
   Author's builds, materials or private Checkpoint content to a tool that does not keep
   them confidential.
5. **The Platform's AI curator** produces feedback that is labelled as such. It never
   replaces your written answer on a Checkpoint you run.

## 10. Restriction, suspension and termination

1. **You may terminate** this Agreement at any time by switching the advisor role off. If
   a Checkpoint is running, you finish it or we replace you under 10.4.
2. **We may terminate** this Agreement for convenience by giving you **30 days'** notice.
3. **We may restrict, suspend or terminate immediately** where you seriously breach this
   Agreement, where the law or an authority requires it, or where there is a real risk to
   users. We will give you a **statement of reasons** and the facts we relied on, and you
   may object by replying to that statement; we answer an objection within 14 days.
4. **Running Checkpoints.** If you stop for any reason inside a running Checkpoint, we may
   assign a replacement curator, tell the Author, and refund the Author for the parts
   nobody performed.
5. Termination does not affect Fees already earned, or the sections that by their nature
   survive (11, 12, 13, 15, 16, 19).
