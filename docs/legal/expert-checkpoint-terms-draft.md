## 1. What you buy

A Checkpoint runs **28 days** on one stage of one project. With a **curator who is a
person** it includes:

1. **Five calls** with your curator, one to one with your team, on days **1, 8, 15, 22
   and 29**. A call is planned for 45 minutes and may run longer; up to ten members of
   your team may join it.
2. **A goal card**, agreed with the curator on the first call: what the project has now,
   the tasks that must be done by the end, and the goal in one sentence.
3. **Call notes** after each call — what was discussed, what was agreed, what is still
   open — written from the call's recording (Section 9).
4. **A verdict** with the curator's note, sent from the last call on day 29 and **by the
   end of day 31**: ready for the next stage, goal met, or goal missed.
5. **A certificate** of the closed Checkpoint, issued to the project and carried by its
   owner, whose name is on it (Section 8).
6. **The gdhub.io tools the program runs on** for its duration — the goal card, the
   devlogs, the evidence slots for each task, the weekly check-ins, the reminders and the
   project's chat with the curator.

**A Checkpoint with the AI curator** has the same goal card, tools, verdict and
certificate, and **no calls**: instead the AI curator writes a review of the team's work
on days **8, 15 and 22** and a final review once the 28 days are over, with the verdict.
