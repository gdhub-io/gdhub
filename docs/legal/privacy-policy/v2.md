## 3. Categories of Data

### 3.1 Public Profile Data

Visible to other users of the Platform:

- First name, last name
- Profile photo
- Skills, experience, portfolio links
- Tools and technologies used
- Availability (time commitment)
- Participation in projects

Purpose: collaboration, team formation, visibility in the community.

### 3.2 Account Data (not public)

- Email address
- Year of birth
- Sign-in data from Google, where you sign in with it
- IP address and technical logs
- Account activity

Purpose: authentication, security, operating the Platform.

### 3.3 Communication Data

- Messages in the Platform's chat
- Telegram / Discord username, where you add it

Purpose: collaboration and communication between users.

### 3.4 Project and Checkpoint Data

- Project pages, devlogs, vacancies and applications
- In an AI Checkpoint: the goal card, devlogs, evidence, weekly check-ins, the AI curator's reviews and the verdict

Purpose: running the Platform's features and the Checkpoint, and keeping the Checkpoint's record and certificate.

### 3.5 Payment Data

Payments are taken by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Your card details go to Paddle and never reach us. We receive the name, email, country, product, amount and transaction reference of a purchase.

Purpose: delivering what you bought, refunds, accounting.

### 3.6 Optional Data

- City / country

Purpose: improving matching and localisation.

## 6. Data Retention

- Account data: while the account is active, and up to 30 days after it is deleted (for recovery and security).
- Content you contributed to shared work (e.g. devlogs, project pages): may be kept in anonymised form, as the Terms of Use describe.
- The record and certificate of a closed Checkpoint: kept after the Checkpoint ends, including after the account is closed, as the Checkpoint Purchase Terms describe.
- Payment and accounting records: for the period tax and accounting law requires.

After that, data is deleted or anonymised.
