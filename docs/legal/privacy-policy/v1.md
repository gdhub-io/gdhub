## 3. Categories of Data

### 3.1 Public Profile Data

This data is visible to other users of the platform:

- First name, last name
- Profile photo
- Skills, experience, portfolio links
- Tools and technologies used
- Availability (time commitment)
- Participation in projects
Purpose: collaboration, team formation, visibility in the ecosystem

### 3.2 Private Data (Restricted Access)

This data is not publicly visible:

- Email address
- Year of birth
- IP address
- Technical logs
- Account activity
Purpose: authentication, security, analytics

### 3.3 Communication Data

- Telegram / Discord username
- Messages or interactions (if applicable in future features)
Purpose: enabling collaboration and communication

### 3.4 Checkpoint Data

When a project runs a Checkpoint:

- the goal card, devlogs, evidence, weekly check-ins and the verdict
- **call recordings (audio) and their transcripts**, made when the curator joins a call; the room says so before anyone joins
- the call notes written from the transcripts
- the names of those who join a call, shown to the curator
Purpose: running the Checkpoint and keeping its record. The recording and the transcript are not shown to anyone; they are kept with the Checkpoint's record unless you ask us to delete them.

### 3.5 Optional Data

- City / country
Purpose: improving matching, localization
