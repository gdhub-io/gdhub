with a curator who is a person on the day of the first call, with the AI curator on the
day you pay or the later day you chose.

Write to {{entity.email}} from the address of your account, or send any other clear
statement that you withdraw. You may use this wording:

payment method you used, without charging you for the refund.

refunds.
