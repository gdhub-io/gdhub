## 1. What is paid

1. **Curation** of a Checkpoint you accepted, under Section 6 of the Advisor Agreement.
2. **Paid Consultations**, once the Platform offers them, under Section 4 below.

Everything else in the advisor role is unpaid, and a Volunteer Engagement under the
Advisor Agreement carries no Fee.

## 2. Your lawful basis and taxes

1. You must have a lawful basis to receive a Fee and to invoice it in your country — in
   Lithuania, individual activity registered with the State Tax Inspectorate
   (individuali veikla) or a business certificate (verslo liudijimas), or a company;
   elsewhere, the equivalent. You tell us which before your first payment, and you tell us
   when it changes.
2. **You are responsible for your own taxes and social contributions** on any Fee, and for
   registering, declaring and paying them. Where Lithuanian law obliges the Platform to
   withhold personal income tax or social contributions from a payment to you, we withhold
   them and pay you the net amount; we tell you before the first such payment.
3. If you are registered for VAT, you invoice with VAT or under the reverse charge, as your
   law requires. Fees are stated exclusive of VAT unless we agree otherwise in writing.
4. Before the first payment you give us your **payment details** — the account holder, the
   account, and how you invoice — and keep them current. We pay nothing to an account that
   is not in your name or your entity's name.

## 3. The Fee for Curation

1. **The Fee is the Checkpoint's price for your level, less the Platform's share**, both as
   published in the curator price list on the day the Author pays. A promotion code the
   Author pays with reduces your Fee in the same proportion as it reduces the price.
2. **It is earned when the Checkpoint is complete:** every call held and the verdict sent.
   You take on the whole Checkpoint, not its parts. Nothing is earned for a Checkpoint you
   decline or do not answer: the Author's money is released, not taken.
3. Where an Author withdraws within the statutory 14 days, or is refunded under the
   [Checkpoint Purchase Terms](/legal/checkpoint-purchase-terms) for what was not performed,
   the Fee is reduced in the same proportion: you are paid for the calls you held and the
   goal card you agreed.
4. **If you stop inside a running Checkpoint** — two missed calls in a row, leaving it, or
   no verdict by the end of day 31 — **no Fee is payable for that Checkpoint**, including
   for the calls you held. We may replace you or close the Checkpoint; the Author is
   refunded for what nobody performed.
5. **A curator who takes over** a running Checkpoint is paid the part of the Fee for the
   calls they hold and the verdict they send.

## 4. Paid Consultations

1. You set the price of your Consultations in your advisor settings, in
   {{entity.currency}}. The Platform shows it to the Author before they book.
2. The Author pays the Platform. The Platform pays you the price you set **less the
   platform fee** published in the advisor settings at the time of booking.
3. A Consultation counts as delivered when it has taken place, or when the Author did not
   attend after a confirmed booking and you were available.
4. Either side may cancel a booked Consultation at least **12 hours** in advance without
   consequence. Later cancellation by you means no Fee for it; later cancellation by the
   Author means the Fee is payable to you.
5. You may switch between free and paid at any time. The change applies to new bookings
   only.

## 5. Payment

1. We pay Fees within **fifteen (15) calendar days** after the end of the calendar month in
   which they were earned, provided you have given us a valid invoice or accepted our
   self-billing document, and valid payment details.
2. We pay in {{entity.currency}}, by bank transfer. Bank charges on your side are yours.
3. We may withhold payment for an engagement that is the subject of a live complaint by an
   Author, until the complaint is decided, and for no longer than 30 days.
4. We may suspend a payment where sanctions, export-control or anti-money-laundering law
   requires it.

## 6. No circumvention

For **six (6) months** after your last paid engagement with a given Author on the Platform,
you will not take that Author off the Platform in order to avoid the Platform's share for
the same type of service you provided here. This does not apply to Authors you already
worked with before, to your generally available services on other channels, or to any
engagement of a different kind.

## 7. Liability

For a paid engagement, your liability under Section 15 of the Advisor Agreement is limited
to the Fee you received for it, except for intent and gross negligence. Our liability to
you includes unpaid Fees in full.

## 8. Changes and termination

1. We notify you of a change to these Terms at least **fifteen (15) days** before it takes
   effect. A Checkpoint already accepted continues under the version in force on the day
   you accepted it.
2. You may stop paid work at any time by declining new Checkpoints and switching paid
   Consultations off. Fees already earned stay payable.
3. These Terms end with the Advisor Agreement.
