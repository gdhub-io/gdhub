This Cookie Policy explains how gdhub.io (the "Platform"), operated by {{entity.name}}, uses cookies and similar technologies.

## 1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. They let the website recognise your browser on the next request, keep you signed in and remember your choices. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage, are covered by this Policy in the same way.

## 2. Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Strictly necessary

These make the Platform work and cannot be switched off. They do not need your consent.

- **Sign-in session** — keeps you signed in and protects sign-in against forgery.
- **Cookie choice** — remembers whether you accepted analytics cookies (stored for one year).
- **Preferences** — e.g. the interface language.

### 2.2 Analytics

Used only if you allow "Analytics" in the cookie banner. Loaded through Google Tag Manager. Each provider sets its own cookies and acts under its own privacy terms.

- **Google Analytics** — tells us which pages are visited and how the Platform is used, in aggregate, so we can improve it (cookies kept for up to two years).
- **Plerdy** — records clicks and scrolling on our pages, without what you type, to show us which parts of a page are used (cookies kept for up to one year).

### 2.3 Marketing

Used only if you allow "Marketing" in the cookie banner, separately from analytics. Loaded through Google Tag Manager, under each provider's own privacy terms.

- **Google Ads** — measures whether our ads on Google lead to visits, sign-ups and purchases, and lets us show our ads on Google to people who have visited the Platform (cookies kept for up to 13 months).
- **Meta Pixel** (Facebook and Instagram) — measures whether our ads on Meta's services lead to visits and sign-ups, and lets us show our ads there to people who have visited the Platform (cookies kept for up to 90 days).
- **Brevo** — connects visits to the Platform with our newsletter, so that the emails we send reflect what was looked at (cookies kept for up to six months).

### 2.4 Support chat

- **Crisp** — the chat window for writing to our team. It remembers your conversation between visits (cookies kept for up to six months). It is loaded once you allow analytics or marketing.

## 3. Your Choice

When you first visit, the cookie banner asks, separately, whether to allow analytics (section 2.2) and marketing (section 2.3) cookies. Both are off until you turn them on. Nothing but strictly necessary cookies is set until you choose. You can change your choice by clearing the Platform's cookies in your browser, which shows the banner again.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

Blocking strictly necessary cookies stops sign-in from working.

Pages that embed third-party content (e.g. a video or a Paddle checkout) may set that service's own cookies, under its own policy. Paddle's checkout uses cookies needed to take the payment securely.
