This Cookie Policy explains how gdhub.io (the "Platform"), operated by {{entity.name}}, uses cookies and similar technologies.

## 1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. They let the website recognise your browser on the next request, keep you signed in and remember your choices. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage, are covered by this Policy in the same way.

## 2. Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Strictly necessary

These make the Platform work and cannot be switched off. They do not need your consent.

- **Sign-in session** — keeps you signed in and protects sign-in against forgery.
- **Cookie choice** — remembers whether you accepted analytics cookies (stored for one year).
- **Preferences** — e.g. the interface language.

### 2.2 Analytics

Used only if you accept them in the cookie banner.

- **Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager** — tells us which pages are visited and how the Platform is used, in aggregate, so we can improve it. Google sets its own cookies for this (for up to two years) and acts under its own privacy terms.

We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies.

## 3. Your Choice

When you first visit, the cookie banner asks whether to allow analytics cookies. Nothing but strictly necessary cookies is set until you choose. You can change your choice by clearing the Platform's cookies in your browser, which shows the banner again.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

Blocking strictly necessary cookies stops sign-in from working.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Pages that embed third-party content (e.g. a video or a Paddle checkout) may set that service's own cookies, under its own policy. Paddle's checkout uses cookies needed to take the payment securely.
