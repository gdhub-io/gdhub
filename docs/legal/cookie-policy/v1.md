## **1. What is Cookie?**

Cookie is a technology that allows a website to store information about your website activity in your browser, which is used by the website while you are using it.

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or other device. These files often consist of data in the form of a chain of numbers and letters, which, for example, identifies your computer or contains other information. Cookies are sent back to the website with each subsequent visit or to another web page that recognizes this cookie. Cookies are used to make websites work or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to website owners. The use of cookies is currently the standard practice for most websites.

Cookies perform many different functions, for example, they allow you to efficiently navigate between webpages, remember your preferences and improve the user interface. Cookies can tell us, for example, whether you have visited a website before or are you a new visitor. They can also help ensure that the ads you see on the Internet are more relevant to you and your interests.

Cookies may be stored on your computer or mobile device for different periods of time. Some cookies are deleted automatically when you close down your browser. Others remain on your device until they expire or you delete them from your cache.

## **3. What Cookies Do We Use?**

3.1. Types of cookies according to their duration

Session cookies. This cookies are stored in the browser only for the duration of your session in the browser, i.e., as long as you leave the website.

Persistent cookies. These cookies are stored in your browser for a set period of time after the end of the session in the browser (if you have not deleted them).

3.2. Types of cookies according to their provenance

First-party cookie. These cookies are stored by our website you are visiting directly. They allow us to collect analytics data, email, name, image and remember language settings, and perform other useful functions that help provide a good user experience, and also allow us to provide you with full functionality of the Service.

Third-party cookies. Third-party cookies are created by domains other than the one you are visiting directly. They are used for cross-site tracking, retargeting and ad-serving.

3.2. Categories of cookies according to their purpose

Strictly necessary cookies. These cookies are essential for you to browse the website and use its features, such as accessing secure areas of the site.

Functionality cookies. These cookies allow a website to remember choices you have made in the past, like what language you prefer, what region you would like weather reports for, or what your user name and password are so you can automatically log in.

Performance cookies. These cookies collect information about how you use a website, like which pages you visited and which links you clicked on. None of this information can be used to identify you. It is all aggregated and, therefore, anonymized. Their sole purpose is to improve website functions.

Marketing cookies. These cookies track your online activity to help advertisers deliver more relevant advertising or to limit how many times you see an ad. These cookies can share that information with other organizations or advertisers.
