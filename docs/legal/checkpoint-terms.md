## 1. What you buy

A Checkpoint runs **28 days** on one stage of one project, and includes:

1. **Five consultations** with your curator — four calls of 45 minutes on days 1, 7, 14
   and 21, and the shared gathering of the season on day 28.
2. **A goal card**, agreed with the curator on day 1: what the project has now, what must
   exist at the end, and the goal in one sentence.
3. **A written answer to each of your four devlogs.**
4. **A result card with a verdict**, published by day 30: goal met and stage closed, goal
   met with the stage open, or goal missed.
5. **A record** of the Checkpoint on the profile of every participant.
6. **The gdhub.io tools the program runs on** for its duration — the goal card, the
   devlogs, the artifact slots, the reminders and the season pages.

The Platform sells and is responsible for the whole of this service, including the part
delivered by software. You have no separate contract with anyone else for any part of it,
and your curator, who is engaged by us, is not your contractual counterparty either.

**A Checkpoint run by the AI curator** contains the same written artifacts — goal card,
four written answers, result card — but **no live calls**: on each session day the AI
curator returns written feedback based on your team's activity. It is offered at a
separate, lower price and is marked as such before purchase.

2. It is not development work. The curator writes no code, draws no art, and does no

3. It is not investment, funding, publishing, an introduction to a publisher, legal advice,
   accounting advice, or a formally accredited qualification.
4. It gives the Platform and the curator **no ownership, equity, revenue share or licence**

5. The curator's advice is professional opinion. Decisions about your project stay yours.

## 5. What you agree to do

1. **Attend the consultations** — you, and up to four members of your team on a call. A
   call may be moved once per Checkpoint by either side, with at least 12 hours' notice.
2. **Publish four devlogs**, each at least 24 hours before its call. No devlog 24 hours
   before a call moves that call once; the second time, the call is lost and is not
   returned.
3. **Attach an artifact to every outcome** on the goal card, as the work lands and in any
   case **by day 26**. An outcome with nothing attached counts as not done, unless the
   curator counts it and writes why.
4. **Confirm once a week**, you and each member, that you are still working on the project.
   Publishing a devlog or attaching an artifact counts as that confirmation.
5. **Give accurate information** about the project, the team and what already exists. The
   goal card rests on it.
6. **Answer for your team.** Members act under your responsibility; you make sure they know
   these Terms, agree to their devlog contributions being published, and behave under the
   Terms of Use.

## 6. The goal and the verdict

1. The outcome list you buy with is a **draft**. On day 1 the curator cuts it to what 28
   days hold, and only then is the goal card fixed. Cutting it there is the curator doing
   their job.
2. The goal card may be changed **once**, on day 14, and every change is recorded beside
   the day-1 wording, never over it.
3. The verdict is measured against the goal card as it stood after day 14, and against
   nothing else. It is written by day 30 and is the curator's professional assessment.
4. **You may not appeal the verdict as such.** You may complain about how the Checkpoint
   was run under Section 13, and you may rate the assessment once it is published.
5. Every verdict leads to a new Checkpoint, bought separately. There is no extension inside
   a Checkpoint.
