If you are a consumer located in the European Union, you have the right to withdraw from a purchase within **14 days** without giving any reason, in accordance with EU Directive 2011/83/EU.

However, for digital content and services that are delivered immediately upon purchase (such as vacancy listings, course access, and event tickets), you explicitly consent to immediate performance and acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal once delivery has begun.
