# Checkpoint Program

The **Checkpoint Program** helps game developers organize their
work around a clear goal and move their project closer to release.

Over 28 days, the **project team** works with a curator to define a
realistic goal, organize the work needed to achieve it, review progress,
and adjust priorities when necessary.

Each sprint begins with a curator session focused on the current state
of the project, the work completed, problems encountered, and what
should be done next. The **project team** documents progress through
devlogs and project artifacts.

By the end of the program, the **project team** will have completed four
sprints of structured development, received regular feedback and
guidance, and produced a concrete result that can become the starting
point of the path to the next Checkpoint.

**For:** Game authors and their project teams
**Duration:** 28 days
**Sessions:** 4 curator sessions
**Devlogs:** 4 progress updates
**Curator:** Industry curator or AI curator
**Outcome:** Project result and final assessment

---

# How the Checkpoint Works

## Focused Development

Work toward one clear and realistic project goal over 28 days.

The Checkpoint helps keep priorities focused on the result the team
wants to achieve and provides a structure for organizing the work around
that goal.

## Curator Guidance

Meet with your curator at the beginning of each sprint to review
progress, discuss problems and decide what deserves attention next.

The curator helps the team keep the work focused, identify blockers, and
set priorities for the next sprint. At the third curator session, the
team also reviews the Checkpoint goal and expected outcomes and adjusts
them where necessary.

\* An AI curator holds no live calls. On each session day it returns
written feedback based on the team's activity during the sprint.

## Devlogs

Document progress throughout the Checkpoint with short devlogs prepared
before the next curator session.

Devlogs keep the work visible, provide context for discussions with the
curator, and create a record of how the project develops over the 28
days.

## Platform Support

The platform helps the team stay active, follow the program rhythm, keep
progress visible, and stay aware of important moments throughout the
Checkpoint.

## Final Review

At the end of the Checkpoint, the curator reviews the final result
against the original goal and provides a final assessment and feedback
on the platform.

# Program Schedule

## Sprint 1 - Define & Start

* Curator session
* Review the current state of the project
* Define the Checkpoint goal and expected outcomes
* Agree on scope and priorities
* Development work
* Upload artifacts for completed outcomes
* Devlog #1

## Sprint 2 - Review & Progress

* Curator session
* Review Devlog #1 and progress
* Discuss problems and blockers
* Set priorities for the sprint
* Development work
* Upload artifacts for completed outcomes
* Devlog #2

## Sprint 3 - Review & Adjust

* Curator session
* Review Devlog #2 and progress
* Review and adjust the Checkpoint goal and expected outcomes
* Set priorities for the sprint
* Development work
* Upload artifacts for completed outcomes
* Devlog #3

## Sprint 4 - Complete

* Curator session
* Review Devlog #3 and progress
* Set priorities for the final sprint
* Development work
* Upload artifacts for completed outcomes
* Final devlog

### Final Assessment

After the final sprint, the curator reviews the final result and leaves
their assessment and feedback on the platform.
