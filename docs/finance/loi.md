# Letters of intent

A letter of intent (LOI) is a signed, non-binding statement that someone means to buy
from, sell through or partner with GDHub on stated terms. For an investor it is the
cheapest evidence that the demand in the [financial model](financial-model.xlsx) is not
invented, so the letters follow the launch order: experts first, then the developers who
would buy from them, then partners who bring both.

| # | Who signs | What it proves | Model input it replaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | An expert (curator) | Supply exists at our prices | `pro_per_curator`, `pro_gmv` |
| B | A developer or team with a project | Someone pays $300–660 for 28 days | `pro_new_m1`, later `ai_new_m1` |
| C | A school, jam, incubator or publisher | Seats come in bulk, acquisition is cheap | `pro_new_m1`, `mkt_fixed` |

**What counts.** A name, a role, a date, a number and a price. "Sounds interesting" in a
chat is not a letter. An email reply that says "yes, I intend to buy one Checkpoint at $340
in December" is.

**Target before a pre-seed conversation:** 10 experts, 30 developers, 3 partners.

---

## A. Expert (curator)

> **Letter of intent — GDHub Checkpoint curator**
>
> I, **[full name]**, **[role, studio, released game]**, intend to curate Checkpoints on
> GDHub (gdhub.io).
>
> - Checkpoints I intend to run in the first three months after launch: **[n]**
> - At most at once: **[n]**
> - Hourly rate I expect: **$[30 / 45 / other]**, knowing the author pays that rate plus
>   GDHub's 25%
> - Genres, engines or stages I am best at: **[…]**
> - I am also interested in paid 1:1 consultations: **[yes / no]**, at **$[…]** an hour
>
> This letter is not binding on either side. It states my intent so that GDHub can plan
> its launch and show its plan to partners and investors.
>
> Signature, date, contact (email, Discord/Telegram)

## B. Developer or team

> **Letter of intent — GDHub Checkpoint**
>
> I, **[full name]**, lead of **[project name, link]**, a team of **[n]** working on
> **[genre, engine, stage]**, intend to buy a Checkpoint on GDHub when it opens.
>
> - Option: **Checkpoint · Pro** with a human curator / **Checkpoint · AI**
> - Price I accept: **$[…]** for 28 days, five calls and a verdict
> - Goal I would set for the first Checkpoint: **[one sentence]**
> - Month I would start: **[month year]**
> - Would I take another Checkpoint after it: **[likely / unlikely / depends on result]**
>
> This letter is not binding and is not a payment. It states my intent so that GDHub can
> plan its launch and show its plan to partners and investors.
>
> Signature, date, contact

## C. Partner (school, jam, incubator, publisher)

> **Letter of intent — partnership with GDHub**
>
> **[Organisation]**, represented by **[name, role]**, intends to work with GDHub as
> follows:
>
> - Kind of partnership: **[seats bought for students or residents / referral of
>   participants / curators from our staff / post-jam continuation for jam teams]**
> - Volume: **[n seats or teams]** per **[month / season / year]**
> - Price or share discussed: **[…]**
> - First Season it would apply to: **[…]**
>
> Non-binding, as above.
>
> Signature, date, contact
