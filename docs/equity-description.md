# Equity Description System Documentation

## Glossary

### Basic Terms
- **Equity** - A share of ownership in the project, represented as a percentage of total project value
- **Allocation** - The distribution of equity among different stakeholders (platform, mentors, investors, team members)
- **Share** - A specific portion of equity assigned to a particular stakeholder

### Team Member Terms
- **Vested Shares** - Equity that is currently owned by a team member and can be transferred or sold
- **Promised Shares** - Future equity that will be granted to a team member upon meeting specific conditions
- **Target Step** - A specific milestone or workflow step that must be completed to vest promised shares
- **Equity Promise** - A commitment to grant a specific percentage of equity to a team member upon meeting certain conditions

### System Terms
- **Platform Share** - Fixed percentage of equity reserved for the platform (3%)
- **Mentor Fund Share** - Percentage of equity reserved for rewarding project mentors
- **Investment Reserve** - Percentage of equity reserved for future investors
- **Team Shares** - Total percentage of equity available for distribution to team members

## For Project Authors

### General Settings
1. Access the equity settings through the "Manage Equity" button in the Team section
2. Configure the following parameters:
   - Platform Share (fixed at 3%)
   - Mentor Fund Share (minimum 2%)
   - Investment Reserve (for future investors)
   - Team Shares (remaining percentage)

### Managing Team Member Equity
1. **Creating Equity Promises**
   - Click on the "Make Promise" button next to a team member
   - Set the percentage of equity to be promised
   - Link the promise to a specific workflow step (optional)
   - Confirm the terms with the team member

2. **Updating Equity Promises**
   - Click on the existing promise percentage
   - Modify the percentage or workflow step
   - Confirm changes with the team member

3. **Deleting Equity Promises**
   - Open the promise modal
   - Click the delete button
   - Confirm that you've discussed the removal with the team member

### Vacancy Equity Settings
1. When creating a vacancy, you can specify:
   - Promise percentage
   - Target workflow step
2. When a candidate is hired, the equity promise will be automatically applied

### Equity Distribution Rules
- Total allocation cannot exceed 100%
- Platform share is fixed at 3%
- Mentor fund must have at least 2%
- Investment reserve can be adjusted
- Remaining shares are available for team members

## For Team Members

### Viewing Your Equity
1. Access the Team section of your project
2. Your equity information is displayed in the Equity Distribution section:
   - Current vested shares (if any)
   - Promised shares (if any)
   - Target workflow step for vesting (if specified)

### Understanding Your Equity
1. **Vested Shares**
   - These are your current equity holdings
   - Displayed as actual percentages in the distribution chart

2. **Promised Shares**
   - These are future equity allocations
   - Will be vested upon reaching specified workflow steps
   - Displayed separately in the distribution chart

3. **Transferring Shares**
   - If you have vested shares, you can transfer them to other team members
   - Use the "Transfer My Share" button
   - Select the recipient and specify the percentage to transfer

### Equity History
- Access the equity transaction history through the history icon
- View all equity-related changes and transfers
- Track when promises were made and shares were vested

## Important Notes
1. All equity changes require confirmation and discussion with affected team members
2. Equity promises are tied to workflow steps and vest upon completion
3. The system maintains a complete history of all equity transactions
4. Total equity allocation must always equal 100%
