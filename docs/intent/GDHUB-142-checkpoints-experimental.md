# GDHUB-142 — Checkpoints say they are experimental and that the AI can be wrong

[Linear](https://linear.app/gdhub/issue/GDHUB-142/checkpoints-say-it-is-an-experimental-feature-and-that-the-ais-answers) · intent written 2026-11-04

## Want

Anyone who meets a Checkpoint knows two things without hunting for them:

- the feature is experimental;
- the AI curator's words are generated by software, can be wrong and are not guaranteed.

Where the AI speaks, a short, quiet line sits under its words, the way ChatGPT and Claude say it under their input box. One link from that line leads to the longer explanation: what is AI-made, that it is not guaranteed, and who to write to.

## Why now

Authors pay for an AI Checkpoint and read its reviews, its goal check and its verdict as a judgement of their project. The Purchase Terms already say the reviews "may be wrong" (§2.5), but nobody reads terms while reading a review. The product itself says nothing, so a wrong verdict reads like a fact.

## Constraints

- **One short line, one component.** The same quiet line, as secondary text with a link, everywhere the AI speaks to the author:
  - the AI curator's message on the checkpoint page;
  - the written sprint review;
  - the goal check advice;
  - the curator note and session body on the workflow path;
  - the verdict, after it lands and on a closed Checkpoint.

  It is shown only when the curator is the AI (`curatorKind === AI`), never under a human curator's words.
- **The human curator's AI brief** gets the same line: it is AI-made too. The verdict modal's AI read keeps its existing subtitle, which already says this.
- **An "Experimental" tag** sits on the Checkpoint entry points, whoever the curator is: the landing hero, the purchase shell's title and the checkpoint page header.
  - Wording: "Experimental", not "Beta". The logo already says beta for the whole platform.
- **The long text is a new FAQ item** on the Checkpoint landing ("Can the AI curator be wrong?"). It has its own anchor so the short line can open it, and it links to the Purchase Terms.
- **No legal text changes.** The Purchase Terms §2.5 and §12.2 already cover it, and this wording must not promise more than they do.
- Copy goes through Tolgee, English only. Design comes first, in Pencil.

## Done when

- [ ] Under every AI-written review, goal check, curator note and verdict the author sees, there is the line "AI can make mistakes. Check important details." and a link to the FAQ item.
- [ ] Under a human curator's words there is no such line.
- [ ] The landing hero, the purchase shell and the checkpoint page header show an "Experimental" tag.
- [ ] The landing FAQ has "Can the AI curator be wrong?", and the line's link scrolls to it and opens it.
- [ ] The human curator's call brief shows the same line.

## How to check it on the platform

1. `/dev-signin` → an author with a running AI Checkpoint.
   - Open the project's workflow: the curator note shows the line.
   - Open the checkpoint page: the header has "Experimental", and the AI message, the review tab and the goal card show the line.
2. Click the line's link. You land on `/checkpoint#…` with the FAQ item open.
3. An author with a closed AI Checkpoint: the verdict shows the line.
4. An author whose Checkpoint has a human curator: no line under the curator's words, and the "Experimental" tag is still in the header.
5. `/checkpoint` and `/checkpoint/new`: the hero and the purchase title show "Experimental".
6. A human curator persona, Brief tab in the call room: the line is under the brief.

## Out of scope

- How the AI works: prompts, models, confidence.
- The scout's project card. Its AI rows already say "Public game-dev knowledge".
- The admin curator journal and the dev AI-curator lab.
- Changing the Purchase Terms or adding "experimental" to them.

## Open questions

- **Where the link goes.** Assumed: the landing FAQ item, not the terms. The terms are linked from the FAQ answer.
- **"Experimental" on a human-curator Checkpoint.** Assumed: yes. It is the feature that is experimental, not only the AI. Expert Checkpoints are closed for now anyway.
